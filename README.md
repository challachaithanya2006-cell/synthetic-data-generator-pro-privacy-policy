# Privacy Policy for Synthetic Data Generator Pro

Last Updated: June 2026

Synthetic Data Generator Pro is a Microsoft Edge extension designed to generate synthetic and sample data for testing, development, and educational purposes.

## Information Collection

Synthetic Data Generator Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) from users.

## Data Usage

The extension generates synthetic data locally within the user's browser. No user-generated data is sent to external servers unless explicitly stated by the user.

## Third-Party Services

Synthetic Data Generator Pro does not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties.

## Security

We are committed to maintaining the security and privacy of our users. The extension operates with minimal permissions required for its functionality.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
